# OCR word suggestions

Review Desk offers a separate preview/apply action in document and text review modes.

1. Click เสนอแก้คำ OCR to preview recognized word substitutions.
2. Compare the proposed text against the source image.
3. Click ใช้ข้อความที่เสนอ if appropriate. The text remains editable.
4. Check dates, IDs, amounts and punctuation yourself. In document mode, use the existing field suggestion action after correcting text.
5. Confirm against the image and submit a decision separately.

The explicit English vocabulary covers line-start Amourt/Arnrourt → Amount and Serwce → Service. On Service lines only, Synthetx → Synthetic, reper/repew before estimate variants → repair, and estirnate/estirnte → estimate. Other words, including ambiguous I0/1D labels, stay unchanged.

No digits, decimal points, date separators, field values, or original OCR are altered. Applying clears image confirmation and invalidates field previews. Switching cases or changing input invalidates word proposals. Nothing is saved until the reviewer submits a decision.

These are suggestions, not proof of the intended word. This vocabulary was selected from observed errors; no held-out OCR accuracy improvement has been established. It does not train or replace the recognizer. Review remains required.
